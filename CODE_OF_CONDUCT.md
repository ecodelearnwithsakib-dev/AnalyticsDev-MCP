# Code of Conduct

Analytics Dev MCP is built for the Analytics Dev community and the wider MCP ecosystem. We want everyone — beginners and experts, from every country and background — to feel welcome.

## Our standards

Expected:

- Be respectful, patient and kind, especially with people who are new.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; focus on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent and ask before assuming.
- Protect privacy: never share someone else's personal data, credentials or client information.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, and personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, stalking, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing), credentials or confidential client data.
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or deliberately disruptive behaviour.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and community channels — and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub (profile [@ecodelearnwithsakib-dev](https://github.com/ecodelearnwithsakib-dev)) or the repository's private vulnerability report form for anything sensitive. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, depending on how serious and repeated the behaviour is.
